2. About cookies

2.1 A cookie is a small file composed of letters and numbers. It is created by a web server, sent to a web browser, and stored by the browser. Each time the browser requests a page from the server, the identifier from the cookie is sent back to the server.

2.2 Cookies can be classified as either "persistent" or "session" cookies. A persistent cookie remains in your browser until its expiration date, unless it is deleted earlier by you; a session cookie expires at the end of your browsing session, when the web browser is closed.

2.3 While cookies themselves do not typically contain information that personally identifies a user, the personal data we store about you may be linked to the information obtained and stored in cookies.

(c) security – cookies are an integral part of our security measures. They help protect user accounts from fraudulent access and enhance the overall security of our website and services;

(d) advertising – cookies are utilized to display advertisements that are likely to be of interest to you, enhancing your browsing experience;

(e) analysis – we employ cookies to analyze how our website and services are used, assisting in our efforts to improve performance and functionality

(f) cookie consent – we may use cookies to remember your preferences regarding the use of cookies on our website.
