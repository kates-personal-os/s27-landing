03How it works

Why Station is different.

2.5 days that move your business forward, give you momentum for the year ahead, recharge you personally, and create new friendships and partnerships.

80 founders · Two and a half days

Day 1

Build the trust

When everyone has already achieved something exceptional, there is nothing to prove. Guards come down, conversations become honest, and real relationships begin.

Day 2

Solve what matters

Bring the challenge blocking your next stage of growth. Peers help sharpen the problem, challenge assumptions and develop practical ways forward.

Day 3

Commit to action

Pressure-test the strongest path, decide what happens next and leave with clear commitments — from decisions and introductions to concrete next steps.
